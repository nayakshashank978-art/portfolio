Overview

Onions are prone to rotting, sprouting and weight loss in storage because conditions inside traditional sheds are unmonitored. This project designs a low-cost smart shed that senses its environment and acts automatically.

Problem

Humid air and poor ventilation trigger fungal rot, and one bad onion spoils the pile before anyone notices. Small farmers need something affordable, simple, and reliable.

Solution

01Sensors track temperature, humidity and CO₂ inside the onion stack.
02A microcontroller drives exhaust fans and vents based on thresholds.
03Alerts reach the farmer by SMS or app when conditions go out of range.
04Raised, slatted bins with bottom-up airflow keep onions dry and off the floor.

Process

Research — visits to onion farms and interviews. Design — shed layout, airflow sketches, circuit diagram. Build — prototype with ESP32, DHT22 and CO₂ sensors. Test — two-week trial compared against a control shed.
